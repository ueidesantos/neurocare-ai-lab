# Project Mission and Ethical Guidelines

## Overview
The **neurocare-ai-lab** is an educational Generative AI and Software Engineering laboratory developed for postgraduate studies in Artificial Intelligence. It serves as a practical environment to explore the intersection of structured health data, deterministic logic, and Large Language Models (LLMs) within a framework of **Responsible AI**.

The project simulates a Minimum Viable Product (MVP) titled **"Cognitive Report Generator for Consultation"**.

## Core Mission
The primary mission of this repository is educational. It aims to demonstrate how to build software that handles sensitive topics with strict ethical guardrails, prioritizing safety, auditability, and clear boundaries between human-provided data, deterministic logic, and generative outputs.

## Responsible AI & Ethical Boundaries
This project is a **learning lab**, not a medical product, diagnostic tool, or commercial healthtech.

### Strict Prohibitions
To maintain ethical standards and ensure safety, the system:
- **NEVER** diagnoses Alzheimer’s, dementia, or any other clinical condition.
- **NEVER** calculates individual disease probabilities.
- **NEVER** recommends specific treatments or medications.
- **NEVER** prescribes exams or medical procedures.
- **NEVER** replaces a formal medical evaluation by a qualified professional.

### Non-Diagnostic Nature
All reports generated by this system are strictly **non-diagnostic**. They are designed to organize and summarize information provided by caregivers or family members to facilitate a more productive discussion with a healthcare professional.

## How It Works
1. **Structured Data Collection:** The system collects responses from caregivers or family members regarding memory, behavior, functionality, and potential confounding factors.
2. **Deterministic Triage:** Classification of "attention priority" is handled exclusively by deterministic, hard-coded rules.
3. **Generative Synthesis:** AI is used only to summarize, organize, and improve the clarity of the report. It does not contribute to the "judgment" or "triage" logic.
4. **HCP-Centric:** The final output is intended solely as a supporting document for a patient's doctor.

## Educational Goals
- Implementing **Clean Architecture** in .NET 10.
- Applying **Responsible AI Guardrails**.
- Practicing **Deterministic vs. Probabilistic** logic separation.
- Managing **Synthetic Data** for privacy-safe development.
